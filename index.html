Protein, carbs, and fat are calculated from calorie shares using 4, 4, and 9 calories per gram. Macro starting ranges reference adult macronutrient distribution ranges. Calorie estimates use the Mifflin–St Jeor equation; goal settings apply a 10% adjustment as a simple starting point.

This is an estimate, not medical advice. Your actual needs can differ. For adults 18+ only; not intended for pregnancy, breastfeeding, or managing a medical condition. Consider a qualified health professional for personal guidance. See NIDDK's guidance.

How the calorie estimate works

Resting energy is estimated with the Mifflin–St Jeor equation using age, height, weight, and the selected formula setting. An activity factor estimates daily energy needs. The goal setting applies a modest adjustment to that estimate.

Use it as a starting point

Calorie equations predict averages and can be off for an individual. Real-world weight trends over several weeks are more useful than any single estimate. Adjust gradually and seek professional advice when appropriate.
